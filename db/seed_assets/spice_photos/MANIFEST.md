# Spice product photo manifest

These 8 images replace the earlier Wikimedia-sourced set (archived, not deleted, at
`../spice_photos_legacy/` — see that folder's own `MANIFEST.md`). User-provided on
2026-09-16 (dropped into `demo_ecom_api/images/`, moved here and renamed by filename
content since the originals were generically named `Code_Generated_Image*.png`).

no visible branding/packaging/text on any of them.
